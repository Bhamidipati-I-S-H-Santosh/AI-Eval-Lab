# Northwind Labs — Benefits Overview

## Learning budget
Every employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books,
certifications, and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Wellness
The company pays up to $60 per month toward a gym membership or fitness app.
Employees also get free access to the CalmMind meditation app.

## Retirement
Northwind Labs matches retirement contributions up to 5% of base salary.
Matching contributions vest fully after 2 years of employment.

## Equipment refresh
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Employees can request an early refresh
if their device fails a hardware diagnostic.
