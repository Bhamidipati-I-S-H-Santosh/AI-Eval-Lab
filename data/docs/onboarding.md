# Northwind Labs — Onboarding Guide

## First week
On day one, new hires collect their laptop from the IT desk on the 3rd floor
and complete security training in the LearnHub portal. Security training must
be finished within the first 5 working days.

## Buddy program
Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team for
their first 90 days. Buddies meet with the new hire at least once a week.

## Probation
The probation period is 3 months. At the end of probation, the manager holds
a review meeting and confirms the employment in PeopleHub.
