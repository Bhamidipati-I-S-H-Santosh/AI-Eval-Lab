# Northwind Labs — Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses
Expenses are submitted in the SpendWise app within 30 days of the purchase.
Every expense above $25 needs a receipt. Reimbursements are paid with the next
monthly payroll run.

## Travel
Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed
for flights of 6 hours or longer, with VP approval. Hotels are capped at $220
per night in standard cities and $320 per night in high-cost cities such as
New York, London, and Singapore.

## Meals
The daily meal allowance during business travel is $75. Alcohol is not
reimbursable unless it is part of an approved client entertainment event.

## Home office
Each employee may claim a one-time home office allowance of $500 for a desk,
chair, or monitor, plus $40 per month toward internet costs.
