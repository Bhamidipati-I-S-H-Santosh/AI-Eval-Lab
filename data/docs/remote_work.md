# Northwind Labs — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule
Employees in hybrid roles work from the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone.

## Working abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year.
Requests must be submitted to HR at least 3 weeks before travel, and some
countries are excluded for tax and legal reasons.

## Fully remote roles
Fully remote employees must attend the annual company offsite, which takes
place every October. Travel costs for the offsite are covered by the company.
