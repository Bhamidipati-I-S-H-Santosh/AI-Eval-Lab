# Northwind Labs — Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 8 unused days can be carried
over into the next year; carried-over days expire on March 31.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for any sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must be taken
within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## How to apply
All leave requests are submitted through the PeopleHub portal. Annual leave
longer than 5 days must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance and approved
by your direct manager.
