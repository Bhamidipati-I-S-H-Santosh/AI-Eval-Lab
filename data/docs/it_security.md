# Northwind Labs — IT Security Policy

## Passwords and MFA
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication
(MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts, using the Authy app or a hardware
security key. SMS codes are not accepted as a second factor.

## Devices
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after
5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system updates must be installed within
7 days of release.

## Reporting incidents
Suspected phishing emails are reported with the "Report Phish" button in
Outlook. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@northwindlabs.example
within 1 hour of discovery.

## Data classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Restricted
data, such as customer payment information, may never be pasted into external
AI tools.
